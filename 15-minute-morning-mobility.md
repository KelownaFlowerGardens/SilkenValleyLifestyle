A short mobility routine can make the rest of the day feel easier.

## Routine
- Neck and shoulder rolls — 1 minute
- Cat-cow — 2 minutes
- Hip circles — 2 minutes
- Easy squats — 3 minutes
- Hamstring and calf stretches — 5 minutes
- Slow breathing — 2 minutes

Move within a comfortable range and stop if anything hurts.
