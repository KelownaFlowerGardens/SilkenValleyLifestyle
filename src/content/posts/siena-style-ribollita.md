# Siena-Style Ribollita

There is nothing quite like a hearty bowl of soup on a cold winter day. This rustic Tuscan soup brings together tender beans, seasonal vegetables, and Parmesan, slowly simmered until rich and velvety. Pieces of rustic bread melt into the broth, creating a wonderfully thick, comforting texture that warms you from the inside out.

In Siena, the soup is traditionally finished with a golden crust of caramelized onions and bubbling Parmesan, much like a French onion soup. It is my favourite way to serve it, especially on a chilly winter evening. Generous and wonderfully satisfying, this recipe is perfect for sharing around the table with family and friends.

## Recipe Details

- **Serves:** 4 to 6
- **Cuisine:** Italian-inspired
- **Category:** Soups & Main Course

## Ingredients

### For the Ribollita

- 4 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1 cup chopped yellow onions
- ½ cup chopped fennel or celery
- ½ cup chopped carrots
- 2 garlic cloves, chopped
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1 (15-ounce) can cannellini or butter beans, undrained
- 1 large bunch lacinato (Tuscan) kale, tough ends trimmed and chopped
- 2 cups low-sodium beef broth
- ½ cup passata (tomato purée)
- 2-ounce chunk Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese, broken into pieces
- 2 ciabatta rolls, preferably a day or two old, cubed (about 2 cups)
- 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
- 1 small red onion, very thinly sliced
- ⅓ cup freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese
- Fennel fronds, for garnish
- Additional olive oil, for serving

### For the Brown Rice

- 2 cups brown rice
- 4 cups water

### For the Pork Marinade

-1 cup olive oil
-½ cup red wine vinegar
-1 tablespoon minced fresh basil
-½ teaspoon oregano
-½ teaspoon thyme
-1 tablespoon minced garlic
-⅛ teaspoon red pepper flakes

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Pork Marinade

Thaw the pork if necessary. In a separate bowl, combine the olive oil, red wine vinegar, fresh basil, oregano, thyme, garlic ,red pepper flakes. Add the pork and turn to coat all surfaces.

Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.

### 2. Prepare the Vegetables

In a Dutch oven, heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil over medium-low heat. Add the yellow onions, fennel, carrots, garlic, and salt and pepper to taste.

Sauté until softened, about 6 minutes.

### 3. Cook the Beans and Kale

Add half the beans to the pot and use a potato masher to crush them into a rough purée.

Add the remaining beans and their liquid, the kale, broth, passata, Parmigiano pieces, and 2 cups of water.

Bring to a boil. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes.

### 4. Preheat the Oven

Preheat the oven to 450°F.

### 5. Cook the Pork

Heat a little oil in a medium frying pan over high heat. Once hot, add the marinated pork.

Cook for approximately 2 minutes on each side, then reduce the heat to medium. Cover and continue cooking until completely cooked through, approximately 15 minutes. Set aside.

### 6. Add the Bread

Stir the bread cubes into the Dutch oven, making sure they are submerged in the liquid.

Cover and simmer until the kale is very soft and the bread begins to fall apart, about another 15 minutes.

### 7. Cook the Rice

Add the brown rice and water to a pressure cooker. Cook according to the manufacturer's instructions, or approximately 15 minutes if appropriate for your pressure cooker.

### 8. Create the Soup's Texture

Stir the soup briskly with a whisk to break up the bread cubes and cheese, creating a thick, porridge-like texture.

Stir in the red wine vinegar and season with additional salt and pepper to taste.

### 9. Prepare the Topping

Arrange the red onion slices over the soup in a single layer and sprinkle evenly with the freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano.

Drizzle with the remaining 2 tablespoons of olive oil.

### 10. Bake Until Golden

Transfer the Dutch oven to the preheated oven and bake uncovered until the onion and cheese are golden brown, about 20 minutes.

### 11. Serve

Spoon the brown rice into serving bowls and add the marinated pork. Ladle the hot Ribollita over the top, making sure each serving receives some of the golden onion and Parmesan crust.

Finish with an additional drizzle of olive oil and garnish with fennel fronds.
