A hearty, hands-off dinner with tender sirloin tip roast, baby potatoes, carrots, and a savoury broth. 
## Recipe 

- **Prep time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook time:** 5-6 hours on High
- **Total time:** About 8 hours 10 minutes or longer
- **Servings:** 6
- **Equipment:** Large slow cooker

## Ingredients

- 3 lb (about 1.4 kg) sirloin tip roast, fully thawed, with twine removed
- 1 lb russet potatoes
- 2 cups diced carrots
- 2 small yellow onion, sliced into rings
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter, cubed
- 1 cup beef broth
- 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried parsley
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. Lay the potatoes carrots, and onion in the bottom of the slow cooker. Scatter butter cubes over the vegetables and place the roast on top.
2. In a small bowl, whisk together the spices and beef broth, apple cider vinegar, soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, add salt and pepper.
3. Gently pour the broth mixture over the roast. Cover with the slow-cooker lid.
4. Cook until the beef is fork-tender: approximately 8–10 hours on Low. For a faster option, cook for 5-6 hours on High; the texture may be less tender.
5. Shred the beef with two forks in the slow cooker. Gently mix it with the vegetables and cooking juices, then serve.

## Optional Gravy

For a thicker gravy, mix 2 tbsp cornstarch with 2 tbsp cold water until smooth. Stir it into the cooking liquid and cook, covered, on High for about 15 minutes.

## Storage

Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for 3–4 days. The original author notes that cooked potatoes may change texture after freezing.
