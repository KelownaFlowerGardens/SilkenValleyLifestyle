A weekly reset works best when it’s small enough to repeat.

## Weekly reset
- Clear surfaces and put items back where they belong
- Wipe kitchen counters and sink
- Do a quick bathroom refresh
- Vacuum or sweep high-traffic areas
- Empty trash and recycling
- Choose one small extra task (fridge shelf, drawer, or entryway)

Keep the routine flexible so it supports your household, not the other way around.
