# Weekly Routine

**Sunday: Deep Clean**
Start the week off with everything fresh, because you deserve the peace of mind to start your week off right.

**Monday: Clean Bedrooms**
Vacuum, sheets, windows, surfaces.

**Tuesday: Laundry\***
Wash colours, blacks. Also clean all the bathrooms.

**Wednesday: Kitchen Reset**
Clean toaster crumbs, empty fridge leftovers, clean stovetop, wash sink stoppers in dishwasher, clean out compost bin, clean out garbage/recycling bin and add new bags, wipe down surfaces, fill up soap containers (dish/hand), refill cleaning wipe containers and paper towels.

**Thursday: Laundry\***
Wash whites, throw blankets, towels, house robe, dish clothes, hand clothes and face cloths.

**Friday**
Wash sheets, pillow cases, throw blankets.

## Everyday Reminders

- Go to bed with a clean kitchen, no dishes left out or in the sink
- Dishwasher on
- Laundry/dryer on
- Check/refill of toilet paper roll holders
- Switch out dish cloths
- Refill tissue paper holders
- Grind coffee
- Refill water coolers in gym
- Fold throw blankets/fluff pillows daily
