This one-pan meal is built for busy evenings.

## Ingredients
- 2 chicken breasts or thighs
- 1 lemon
- Olive oil, salt, pepper
- Garlic and dried herbs
- Vegetables of choice

## Steps
1. Season the chicken and vegetables.
2. Roast or sauté until cooked through.
3. Finish with lemon juice and herbs.

Adjust the vegetables based on what you have on hand.
