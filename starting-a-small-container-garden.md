Container gardening works well on patios, balconies, and small yards.

## Getting started
1. Choose containers with drainage holes.
2. Use potting mix suited to your plants.
3. Start with easy options like herbs, lettuce, or cherry tomatoes.
4. Place containers where they get the right amount of sun.
5. Water consistently, checking soil moisture regularly.

Start small and expand as you learn what thrives in your space.
