Container gardening works well on patios, balconies, and small yards.

## Getting started
1. Choose containers with drainage holes.
2. Use potting mix suited to your plants.
3. Start with herm mixtures that compliment each other. I use a) Sage b) Rosemary c) Chamomile in a pot together. While, Basil and Mint are grown seperately.
4. Place containers where they get the right amount of sun.
5. Water consistently, checking soil moisture regularly. Making sure the water catchment tray beneath never goes dry.
