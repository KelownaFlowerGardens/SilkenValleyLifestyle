Consistent routines help pets feel secure and make care easier to manage.

## Weekly checklist
- Refresh water bowls and clean food areas
- Check grooming needs (brushing, nails, coat)
- Wash bedding or favorite resting spots
- Review food, treats, and litter/supplies
- Set aside play, training, or enrichment time

Adjust the checklist to your animal’s species, age, and health needs.
