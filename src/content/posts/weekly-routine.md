<h1>Weekly Routine</h1>
<div class="rule"></div>

<div class="day"><b>Sunday: Deep Clean</b><p>Start the week off with everything fresh, because you deserve the peace of mind to start your week off right.</p></div>
<div class="day"><b>Monday: Clean Bedrooms</b><p>Vacuum, sheets, windows, surfaces.</p></div>
<div class="day"><b>Tuesday: Laundry*</b><p>Wash colours, blacks. Also clean all the bathrooms.</p></div>
<div class="day"><b>Wednesday: Kitchen Reset</b><p>Clean toaster crumbs, empty fridge leftovers, clean stovetop, wash sink stoppers in dishwasher, clean out compost bin, clean out garbage/recycling bin and add new bags, wipe down surfaces, fill up soap containers (dish/hand), refill cleaning wipe containers and paper towels.</p></div>
<div class="day"><b>Thursday: Laundry*</b><p>Wash whites, throw blankets, towels, house robe, dish clothes, hand clothes and face cloths.</p></div>
<div class="day"><b>Friday</b><p>Wash sheets, pillow cases, throw blankets.</p></div>

<h2>Everyday Reminders</h2>
<div class="rule"></div>
<ul>
<li>Go to bed with a clean kitchen, no dishes left out or in the sink</li>
<li>Dishwasher on</li>
<li>Laundry/dryer on</li>
<li>Check/refill of toilet paper roll holders</li>
<li>Switch out dish cloths</li>
<li>Refill tissue paper holders</li>
<li>Grind coffee</li>
<li>Refill water coolers in gym</li>
<li>Fold throw blankets/fluff pillows daily</li>
</ul>
