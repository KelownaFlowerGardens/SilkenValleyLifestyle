# The Best Cinnamon Rolls You'll Ever Eat

**Recipe by:** Monique Volz, Ambitious Kitchen
**Source:** https://www.ambitiouskitchen.com/best-cinnamon-rolls/

| | |
|---|---|
| **Prep time** | 2 hours |
| **Cook time** | 20 minutes |
| **Total time** | 2 hours 20 minutes |
| **Makes** | 9 large cinnamon rolls |

Big, fluffy, soft cinnamon rolls with a brown sugar–cinnamon filling and cream cheese frosting. Can be made overnight, made ahead, or frozen.

---

## Ingredients

### Dough
- ¾ cup (180 g) milk (whole or 2%)
- 2¼ teaspoons (one ¼-oz package) quick-rise (instant) or active dry yeast*
- ¼ cup (50 g) granulated sugar
- 4 tablespoons (57 g) salted butter, melted and cooled
- 1 large egg plus 1 large egg yolk, at room temperature
- 3 cups (360 g) bread flour, plus more as needed
- ¾ teaspoon kosher salt
- Extra-virgin olive oil, for greasing the bowl

### Filling
- 4 tablespoons (57 g) salted butter, softened
- ⅔ cup (142 g) packed dark brown sugar
- 1½ tablespoons ground cinnamon

### Cream Cheese Frosting
- 4 oz (112 g) cream cheese, softened
- ¾ cup (85 g) powdered sugar
- 3 tablespoons salted butter, softened
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

---

## Instructions

1. **Warm the milk.** Microwave until it reaches 115°F (about 45–60 seconds). It should feel like warm bath water, not hot.
2. **Mix the dough.** Put the milk in a stand mixer bowl (paddle attachment) and sprinkle in the yeast. If using active dry yeast, add the sugar and let it sit until foamy, about 5 minutes. Add the sugar, melted butter, egg, and yolk, and mix on medium for 30 seconds. Stir in the flour and salt with a wooden spoon until a dough forms.
3. **Knead.** Switch to the dough hook and knead on medium speed for 8–10 minutes, until soft and slightly sticky. If it sticks to the bottom of the bowl, add 1–3 tablespoons more bread flour, one at a time. (You can also knead by hand on a floured surface.)
4. **First rise.** Place the dough in an oiled bowl, cover with plastic wrap and a warm towel, and let rise until doubled, 1–1½ hours. It's ready when a finger poked about an inch in doesn't bounce back right away.
5. **Roll out.** On a generously floured surface, roll the dough into a 10 × 14-inch rectangle.
6. **Fill.** Spread the softened butter over the dough, leaving a ¼-inch margin along the far short side. Mix the brown sugar and cinnamon, sprinkle over the butter, and gently rub it in.
7. **Roll and cut.** Starting from the short side without the margin, roll the dough up tightly and pinch the seam closed. Place seam-side down and trim about ½ inch off each end. Gently push the ends inward to compact the log. Cut into nine 1-inch slices with unflavored dental floss or a serrated knife.
8. **Second rise.** Arrange the rolls in a parchment-lined 9×9-inch (or 11×7-inch) pan. Cover and let rise 45 minutes to 1 hour, until puffy, doubled, and touching.
9. **Preheat** the oven to 350°F while the rolls rise.
10. **Bake** 20–25 minutes, until golden at the edges. Don't overbake, so the centers stay soft. Cool 10 minutes.
11. **Make the frosting.** Beat the cream cheese, powdered sugar, butter, and vanilla on low for 30 seconds, then on medium-high for 1–2 minutes until smooth and fluffy. Spread over the warm rolls and serve.

## Notes

\*If using quick-rise/instant yeast, no proofing is needed. Active dry yeast should be dissolved in the warm milk until bubbly, and the dough may take longer to rise.

### Tips
- Keep milk between 105–115°F. Hotter can kill the yeast.
- Use room-temperature (not melted) butter in the filling so it doesn't leak out.
- Rub the brown sugar into the butter so it's well combined.
- Use room-temperature eggs. To warm them quickly, set them in a bowl of warm water for a few minutes.
- Bread flour gives the softest, fluffiest result. All-purpose flour works but is less fluffy.
- Flour both your work surface and rolling pin.
- If the centers pop up, the dough was likely rolled too tightly or the pan is too small.
- If the dough is too sticky, add flour about 1 tablespoon at a time.
- For smaller rolls, roll the dough up along the long edge instead. Bake time stays about the same.

### Make-ahead (overnight) rolls
After cutting the rolls and placing them in the pan, cover tightly and refrigerate right away, up to 18 hours. In the morning, let them come to room temperature and rise for about 45 minutes to 1 hour, then bake as directed.

### Freezing
- **Before baking:** Freeze the cut, panned rolls after the first rise, well covered. Thaw in the fridge (ideally overnight), then let rise in a warm spot 30 minutes to 1 hour before baking.
- **After baking:** Cool completely, then freeze the whole pan or individual rolls, well wrapped. Thaw at room temperature, then warm at 350°F for 10–15 minutes or microwave individual rolls for 20–30 seconds. Freezing unfrosted and icing later works best.

### Storage
Store covered in the fridge for up to 5 days. Reheat in the microwave in 15-second intervals.

### Variations
- **Dairy-free:** Use almond milk, vegan butter, and vegan cream cheese.
- **Mix-ins:** Chopped nuts, raisins, or pumpkin pie spice in the filling.
- **Doubling:** Use a larger pan.
